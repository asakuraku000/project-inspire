    TESDA: how to start

    1. Open e-tesda.gov.ph and tap Login.
    2. Tap Create an Account and fill in the required fields.
    3. Check your email and activate your account.
    4. Log in, choose a course, and tap Enroll me in this course.

    A certificate of completion comes after you finish a course. A National Certificate needs a face-to-face assessment. TESDA mobile app: search for it in Google Play or the App Store.

    Sign up page How-to guide
    Questions? TESDA Online Program: (02) 8887 7777 · tesdaonlineprogram@tesda.gov.ph · Main site: www.tesda.gov.ph

    ALS: how to join

    1. Visit or contact your nearest DepEd District or Division Office, or ask your barangay for the ALS teacher. You can join any time of the year.
    2. Take the short assessments (Functional Literacy Test) so your teacher knows what you already know.
    3. Make a learning plan with your ALS Learning Facilitator.
    4. Learn with modules and activities. Lessons can be face-to-face, distance, or mixed.
    5. Take the Accreditation and Equivalency (A&E) Test to move up. Passing can lead to Junior High, Senior High, TESDA, college, or work.

    Documents often asked for: PSA birth certificate (photocopy) and a 2x2 ID photo, plus Form 137 if you came from a formal school. Requirements can change, so confirm with your ALS teacher. DepEd Order No. 001, s. 2026 says no fees may be collected for ALS programs.
