    Learn the hub

    Websites use short words on buttons and in directions. Knowing them helps you find what you need. Do the three activities below.

    1. Read the button

    What does each button do? Tap the best answer.

    2. Pick the search words

    Short, clear words find better results. Which words would you type?

    3. Follow the directions

    These are the steps to make an account on e-TESDA. Tap the steps in the right order.
